From Design to Maintenance

Novum Energy Infra designs, manufactures, installs, commissions and maintains electrical panels.

Design & Engineering

From an enquiry or a single-line diagram, a full quotation with the drawing within one day. Then scheme design, GA and internal layout drawings, busbar and cable sizing, protection coordination and the bill of materials.

Manufacturing

Panels are made at our facility in Balanagar, Hyderabad, with in-house quality control at every stage.

Installation & Commissioning

Installation, termination, testing, commissioning and energisation at site, as an A-Grade electrical contractor. The licence is issued by APELB and is valid in Andhra Pradesh.

Operations & Maintenance

Annual maintenance contracts, preventive maintenance, retrofits and upgrades.

Quotation in one day.

Send an enquiry or a single-line diagram. Our design team works out the requirement and returns a full quotation with the drawing within one day.
