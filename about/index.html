About Us

Novum Energy Infra Private Limited is an electrical panel manufacturer headquartered in Hyderabad, and an A-Grade electrical contractor licensed by APELB in Andhra Pradesh. We build for industrial, commercial, utility and infrastructure customers in Telangana and Andhra Pradesh.

We make low-voltage and substation panels: power distribution, motor control, power factor correction, substation protection and custom fabrication. Each is engineered to the customer’s specification and built at our own facility in Balanagar.

Incorporated in 2026, Novum Energy Infra is founded on over two decades of promoter experience in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana’s power sector.
