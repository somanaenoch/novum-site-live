How we work

  1. Design

    From an enquiry or an SLD: a full quotation with the GA drawing within one day. Then busbar and cable sizing and the bill of materials.

  2. Build

    Enclosures fabricated, busbars cut and bent, panels wired and routine tested to IEC standards. At our works in Balanagar.

  3. Install

    Installation, termination, testing and energisation on site. A-Grade licence, APELB, Andhra Pradesh.

  4. Maintain

    Annual maintenance, retrofits and upgrades.
